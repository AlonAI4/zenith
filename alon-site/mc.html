What it does

  • Minimap and world map

    A round minimap with a compass dial and a scale bar, and a full map you drag and zoom. Relief lit from one side makes trees and cliffs stand out, and zoomed in close each block shows its own texture. Explored land is remembered.

  • Any height

    Underground, the maps show the cave layer you are on. On the world map, Page Up and Page Down show any height, 16 blocks at a time, and caves you walk through are remembered.

  • Waypoints

    Name and colour your places, see them in the world with their distance, and find where you last died.

  • Radar

    Mobs as coloured dots that merge into one when crowded, other players as their face, and a search that points you to a player.

  • Mob health and armour

    Each mob's type, with one heart for its health and one icon for its armour. Look at it in spy mode, or click it on the world map, for its full rows of hearts and armour.

  • Player cards

    On the world map, players near you get a card with their face, name, hearts and armour. Click it for the spy panel: what they wear and hold, their game mode and ping.

  • Eye modes

    Night vision, full bright, spy with target boxes that show each mob's health and armour and a sonar sweep, X-ray, FLIR heat vision and an old CRT screen. Each has its own key.

  • Teleport in creative

    Double-click a spot on the map to go there, landing on the highest safe block. Only in creative mode, where you may use the /tp command.

Read this before you use it online. X-ray, the mob and player radar, the cave map and the world map's caves at any height, the health and armour of players and mobs, target boxes and FLIR outlines through walls break the rules of many servers, and using them there can get you banned. Many servers also hide their ores from mods like this: there, X-ray can show fake ores that are not really there, or miss real ones. Use these in your own worlds, or on servers that say they are allowed. Servers can switch the radar off, which also hides the cards, mob health and armour, target boxes and FLIR outlines, and can switch the cave map off on its own, which leaves the world map on the surface. X-ray, and target boxes and FLIR outlines through walls, are off on a server unless it allows them; in your own worlds they always work.

Install it

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before installing mods.

  1. Install Fabric Loader for your Minecraft version. Download the installer from fabricmc.net, run it, choose Minecraft 26.2 or 26.3 and press Install. The Minecraft launcher then has a fabric-loader profile.
  2. Download Fabric API for the same version from Modrinth. Pick a file marked for it.
  3. Put both files in your mods folder. The Fabric API file and the Zenith MC file go in the mods folder of your Minecraft folder: on a Mac ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/mods, on Windows %appdata%\.minecraft\mods. Make the mods folder if it is not there.
  4. Start Minecraft with the Fabric profile. Then press M for the world map, B to add a waypoint and N for the settings. G cycles the eye modes, or press one key for each: V night vision, J full bright, K spy, R X-ray, ; FLIR, H CRT. Every one of these keys can be changed in Controls. On the world map, Page Up and Page Down, or the arrows at the top right, change the height it shows. The keys are where they are on a US keyboard; on other keyboards, Controls shows their names on yours.
  • Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3. 26.4 comes once Mojang releases it.
  • Only you need it. It runs in your game; servers do not need anything installed.
  • Works with Sodium and Iris. Tested with Sodium 0.9.2 and Iris 1.11.4 on 26.2, Sodium 0.9.3 (beta) and Iris 1.11.7 on 26.3, and with the Complementary Reimagined shader pack on. Iris also uses K and R, and Xaero's maps use M and B: if two mods share a key, change one of them in Controls. G is also Minecraft's own Quick Actions key: on servers that have Quick Actions, one press opens them and changes the eye mode.
